Machinery

The equipment behind our production floor.

Atta Packaging Machine

An automated vertical system used for measured filling and sealing of atta packs.

Entolator

A centrifugal impact machine used to treat flour before storage or packing by disrupting insects and insect eggs.

Roll Body (Roller Mill)

Its rollers grind wheat in stages and help produce flour with a controlled, consistent particle size.

Horizontal Chakkis

Used to crush cleaned, fresh wheat and produce Chakki Fresh Atta in the mill's grinding process.
